Cross
the street

The single most useful skill in Hà Nội. Watch her do it.

1. SlowWalk at a steady, boring pace.
2. SteadyNever stop dead in the middle.
3. ForwardNever step back. That’s what causes accidents.
4. LookEyes on the bikes, and on the pavement too.

Good
to know

Collected from travellers on forums, newspapers and our own front desk.

1

Crossing the street

Walk slowly and steadily. Don’t run, don’t stop, don’t step back. The bikes flow around anyone predictable.

2

Getting around

Use Grab, Xanh SM or Be. The price is fixed before you ride. Rain and rush hour mean surge pricing.

3

From the airport

Book our airport taxi before you fly: 300k by day (8:00–17:00), 350k at night, straight to our door. Send us your flight number on WhatsApp.

4

SIM card

Get one at our desk: 300k for a month of data, and we’ll help you set it up. Bring your passport; sign-up now includes a quick face scan.

5

Cash and ATMs

Street food is cash only. Most ATMs charge ~50–80k a withdrawal, so take more, less often. Always pick VND when the ATM offers to convert. We change cash at the desk too.

6

Tipping

Not expected at street food. Round up at sit-down restaurants; 10–20k for a helpful driver.

7

Haggling

Normal at markets and street stalls: start around half, walk away politely. Not in shops with price tags.

8

Temples

Shoulders and knees covered. Hat off. Shoes off where you see a pile of them.

9

Weather

Oct–Nov and Mar–Apr are the sweet spot. Dec–Feb is grey and damp, sometimes under 10°C: bring a jacket. May–Sep is 30–35°C with storms.

10

Water

Don’t drink the tap. Bottled or filtered only.

11

Renting a motorbike

Legal riding needs a home motorbike licence plus a 1968-convention IDP. Most US, UK, Australian and Canadian permits (1949) don’t count. Fines are 2–4M and insurance often won’t pay. Our tours come with drivers.

12

Your phone

Hold it on the side away from the road. Snatching from passing bikes is the main risk here; violent crime is rare.

13

Tết 2027

Lunar New Year is 6 Feb 2027. Around 4–10 Feb many family eateries close and buses sell out. Book early: we sell bus tickets at the desk.

Scams
to dodge

None of them are dangerous. All of them are annoying.

NOPE

Shoe shine

They start brushing before you’ve agreed, then ask for a fortune. Say “không” and pull your foot away.

NOPE

Fruit-basket photo

A “free” photo with the shoulder pole, then fruit at ten times the price.

NOPE

Cyclo surprise

“50k” turns into per hour, per person, or dollars. Agree price, route, time and currency first, and say it back.

NOPE

Rigged taxis

In town, use Grab or Xanh SM, never a guy who walks up to you. From the airport, book our taxi ahead and skip the haggling.

NOPE

Note switching

500k and 20k notes are both blue; 100k and 10k look alike. Say the amount out loud as you pay.

NOPE

No prices on the menu

Ask “bao nhiêu tiền?” before you order, and check the bill. Wet towels can cost a few thousand.
