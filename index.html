House
rules

The short version. The full poster is on every floor.

1

Quiet hours: 10 PM – 8 AM

Keep voices, music and phone calls low. Our neighbours and sleeping guests will thank you.

2

No food in the dorm.

Please eat in the common area. Otherwise you might wake up with some tiny ant roommates.

3

No smoking or vaping indoors.

Use the designated outdoor area.

4

No outside guests in dorms.

Visitors stay in common areas unless reception says OK.

5

Lock your valuables.

Use your locker. The hostel isn't responsible for unsecured belongings.

6

Lost key / key card: fee applies.

Keys carry a 100,000₫ deposit, refunded at check-out. Lose it and the deposit stays with us.

Thank you for helping us keep the hostel comfortable for everyone.

Asked
a lot

When can I check in and out?

Check-in 14:00 – 23:00. Check-out by 12:00. Reception is open 24 hours.

Can I leave my bag before or after?

Yes, there's luggage storage at the front desk.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, free for every guest, 7:30 – 10:00.

Towels?

Included in the private rooms. In the dorms, rent one for 30,000₫.

Is there a locker?

Yes, every dorm bed has a locker underneath.

Is there a women-only dorm?

Not right now. All five dorms are mixed. Every bunk has its own privacy curtain.

I'm landing at 2 AM. Is that OK?

Reception is open 24 hours. Check-in runs 14:00 – 23:00, so if you'll be later, message us your arrival time.

Can you pick me up from the airport?

Yes. Airport taxi is 300,000₫ from 8 AM to 5 PM, 350,000₫ from 5 PM to 8 AM. Door to door.

Card or cash?

Both. Card payments carry a 5% surcharge. There's currency exchange at the desk.

Is there a deposit?

100,000₫ per key, refunded at check-out.

Stairs or a lift?

There's a lift, so no hauling your backpack up five floors. Dorms are on floors 2, 3 and 5; private rooms on 4.

Laundry?

50,000₫ per kg, or 60,000₫ per kg express.

Can I get a local SIM?

Yes, at the desk: 300,000₫ for a month.
